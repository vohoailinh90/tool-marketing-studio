The hard part of a meeting across 3 countries is not busy calendars. It is that working hours barely overlap 🌏

Try it: Tokyo, Ho Chi Minh City and Prague, everyone working 09:00–18:00. In winter all three are at work together for just 1 hour a day (17:00 Tokyo = 09:00 Prague). A 90-minute meeting? No slot. Add someone in the US and nothing is shared.

(That is a worked example from standard hours with empty calendars. Real teams also have busy diaries and holidays.)

I am prototyping a small tool for this, but it is not released. I would rather hear how you handle it first. How does your team pick a time across time zones? Answer A / B / C / D in the comments 👇
