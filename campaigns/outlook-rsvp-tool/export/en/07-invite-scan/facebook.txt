Click Scan Inbox, get a table of who said Yes, No or Maybe. That's the first part of the tool 📥

How it works: the invite carries Outlook's voting buttons — Yes / No / Maybe — so nobody types a reply. Scan Inbox then reads replies from every folder of your mailbox and counts them. Disagree with a vote? Correct it by hand; later scans keep your edit unless that person sends a newer reply.

Why bother? Because “Sure!”, “Probably” and “Let me check my calendar” don't count themselves.

Honest limit: 2 people sharing one e-mail address can't be told apart.
