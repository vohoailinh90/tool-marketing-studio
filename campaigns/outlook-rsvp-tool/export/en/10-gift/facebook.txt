Gift money gets its own tab — who chipped in, what the gift cost, what's left 🎁

Tick who contributed and double-click to type the real amount. Enter the gift's name, order link and price and the tool works out the remaining money. Want one summary with the dinner? Tick “Add the party's money”.

The report is an HTML e-mail (aligned table, clickable order link) with a spreadsheet of contributors only. You pick who gets it — they don't have to be contributors.

In a group chat the answers end up scattered. How do you collect gift money today? 👇
