Vietnam, India and South Korea are only a few hours apart, yet finding a time that suits everyone is still hard 🌏

Working 09:00–18:00, the three countries overlap for 5.5 hours a day. Roomy, right? But with 10 people, each busy about 30% of their working time, my simple simulation found a 60-minute slot where everyone is free in only about 14% of weeks (with 3 people, almost always).

Add a few managers, booked in different slots, and even 10 people in the same department and time zone have a 60-minute slot where all are free in only about 7% of weeks (about 28% if no one is busier).

Then add public holidays that differ by country, India's 30-minute offset that makes hand conversion error-prone, and calendars outside your organisation that are usually not visible.

(That is a simulation with random calendars, not a real team.)

I am prototyping a small tool for this, but for now it only reads calendars of people in the same organisation and it is not released. I would rather hear how you handle it first. How does your team pick a time across time zones? Answer A / B / C / D in the comments 👇
