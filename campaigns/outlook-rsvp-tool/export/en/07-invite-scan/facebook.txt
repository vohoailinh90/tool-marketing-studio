“Sure!”, “Probably”, “Let me check my calendar”… counting replies like these can eat a whole evening 😵

I used to open every email, type who's in, who's out and who's “maybe” into a spreadsheet, then start over when new replies landed.

So the first step of the tool is to stop asking for typed replies. The invite carries Outlook's voting buttons — Yes / No / Maybe — and recipients just click one. Then I click Scan Inbox: it reads replies from every folder in the mailbox and turns them into a table. Any vote can be corrected by hand, and the correction is kept on later scans.

Honest limit: 2 people sharing one e-mail address can't be told apart.

How many uncounted replies are sitting in your inbox right now? 😅
