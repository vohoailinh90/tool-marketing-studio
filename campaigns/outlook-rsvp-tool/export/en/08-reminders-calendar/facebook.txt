“I already replied!” — what every organiser hears right after sending a reminder 😅

Nudge the whole group and the early repliers get annoyed; stay quiet and people go missing. The hard part is remembering who answered and who didn't.

In the tool, after I click Scan Inbox I can send the reminder only to people who haven't replied, and the calendar invite only to people who answered Yes or Maybe. Both only go out once the open event has been scanned, so they can't land on another event's list.

Honest limit: reminders are sent by hand from the app. Scheduled, automatic reminders aren't built yet.

Would you want them? Comment “REMIND” if so 👇
