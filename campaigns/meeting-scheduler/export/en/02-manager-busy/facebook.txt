Managers fully booked, and spread across countries too: how do you land a time? I simulated 4 ways 🗓️

Setup: 10 people in Vietnam, India and South Korea; 8 busy about 30% of their working time and 2 managers twice as busy (about 60%). The chance that a week has a 60-minute slot where everyone is free is only about 3% (one department in one country: about 7%). On top of who is busy, everyone must also fall inside their own country's working hours. Then each way (3 countries / one country):

1) Make only the essential people must-attend, managers join if they can: about 39% / 61% (if at least 1 of the 2 managers must also be free: about 20% / 35%).
2) Make it 30 minutes instead of 60: about 36% / 65%.
3) Search 4 weeks instead of 1 week: about 10% / 30% (2 weeks: about 7% / 14%).
4) Ask just one person to move one meeting: about 32% / 57% of weeks have a slot where at most one person is not free, and in about 55% / 48% of such slots that person is a manager, so you know who to ask.

Overall, every way helps less once people are in different countries. One tip that needs no simulation: offer 2–3 options to pick from instead of asking each person every time.

(That is a simulation with random calendars; "managers twice as busy" is my assumption, not a measurement of a real team.)
