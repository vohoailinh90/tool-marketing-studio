Farewell gift: who chipped in, how much, what did the gift cost, what's left over? 🎁😵

In a group chat the answers end up scattered, and reporting back means scrolling the thread from the top.

In the tool, gift money has its own tab: tick who contributed, double-click to type the real amount, enter the gift's name, order link and price, and it works out the gift's remaining money. Want one summary? Tick “Add the party's money” to fold the dinner money in.

The report e-mail is an HTML table with a clickable order link, plus a spreadsheet listing only the contributors — and you choose who receives it.
