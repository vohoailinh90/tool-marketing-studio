One column of money per round — that's how the tool handles a dinner that rolls on to round 2 💰

Each round — round 1, round 2 and so on — has its own attendance and amount columns. Tick who came and the amount fills in from the expected budget; double-click anything that differs. Free exempts someone from every round.

Each round shows attendees, collected, paid out and remaining, and the totals over all rounds sit at the top.

Then the thank-you e-mail goes out with a table of every round's money and the spreadsheet attached.

How many rounds does your team dinner usually have? 👇
