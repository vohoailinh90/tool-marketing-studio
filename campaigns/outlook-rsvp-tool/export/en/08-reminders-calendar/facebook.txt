Reminders without the “I already replied!” 🔔

After a scan, the tool knows who hasn't answered. So:
→ the reminder e-mail goes only to people who haven't replied
→ the calendar invite goes only to people who said Yes or Maybe
→ both only go out once the open event has been scanned, so they can't land on another event's list

Honest limit: the app opens each reminder in Outlook and you check it and click Send. Scheduled, automatic reminders aren't built yet.
