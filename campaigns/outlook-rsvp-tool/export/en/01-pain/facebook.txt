If you've ever been "the one who organises the team party", you know this feeling 😅

The party belongs to everyone. The invites, the head count, chasing people who haven't replied, collecting money, splitting the after-party… usually land on one person.

I've done it more than once, and the hard part was never the evening itself — it was working out who's coming, who hasn't answered and who hasn't paid. So I started building a small tool that runs on Outlook to do the repetitive part.

Before I show it: how do you track who's coming today? Answer A / B / C / D in the comments 👇
