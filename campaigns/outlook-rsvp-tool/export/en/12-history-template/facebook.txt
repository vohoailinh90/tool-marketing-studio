“Where did we go last year?” “How much is left in the department fund?” Every hand-over starts like this 😅

The saddest thing about running events from a spreadsheet is that nothing is kept, so the next organiser starts from scratch.

In the tool, every event stays in History with its Yes/No/Maybe counts and send status. The money columns are calculated: income, expense and balance cover every round and the gift money, and the department fund is the running total of balance from the first event. To repeat a similar event, load the old one, change the Event ID, and the recipient list follows the new ID. Votes, attendance and amounts paid stay with the original event.

It's all one file on your PC, so back it up regularly.
