A few of you asked how it actually works, so here it is, step by step 👇

The key is Outlook's voting buttons: recipients click Yes / No / Maybe right inside the email — no link, no login. Then I click Scan Inbox and every reply lands in a table.

The rest is what I used to do in a spreadsheet: calendar invites for attendees, attendance, money per round, gift contributions, and a report back to the team.

If you're on Windows with Outlook and want to try the beta, comment "BETA".
