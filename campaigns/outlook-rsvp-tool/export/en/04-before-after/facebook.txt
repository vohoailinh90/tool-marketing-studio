Before & after I stopped organising team events in a spreadsheet 📊

Before: counting yes/no in the inbox, reminding everyone (including people who'd replied), typing payments into an ever more colourful file. Now: Scan Inbox builds the table, reminders go only to people who haven't answered, totals per round add themselves up.

The "~3 h 20 min → ~30 min" is my estimate for a ~30-person dinner — not a measurement yet. I'll measure it with the first testers and share the real result.

It doesn't run on a Mac or Outlook on the web yet. If you're on Windows Outlook: what would you want it to do?
