"Why not just use Google Forms?" — fair question 🤔

At work, everyone lives in Outlook. Invites arrive in Outlook, replies go back to Outlook, the calendar is in Outlook. Every extra tool you ask people to open is one more reason to forget to answer.

So when my event spreadsheet grew past the point of readable, I went the other way: let Outlook itself do the repetitive part — invites with vote buttons, reading the replies, reminding the people who haven't answered, tracking the money.
