A team that speaks more than one language — and every invite has to be written twice? 🌏

The tool can send e-mails in English, Japanese, Vietnamese, or bilingual Japanese + English. For the bilingual one you write your note in any language, click one button to copy the note and the event details with a ready-made prompt, and paste it into Microsoft Copilot. Copilot answers with a Japanese and an English version; you paste that back into the app. Each half of the e-mail gets its own greeting, Japanese first, English second.

No API key and no app registration, and you can preview and edit before sending. Honest limit: the copy-paste is a manual step, not automatic translation.
