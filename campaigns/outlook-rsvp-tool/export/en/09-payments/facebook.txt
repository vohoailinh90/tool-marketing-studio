Round 2: who paid, who's exempt, who left early… is your colour-coded spreadsheet holding up? 😅

Money across several rounds is where errors creep in. You keep asking: did they pay for round 1, are they coming to round 2, who's exempt?

In the tool, every round has its own attendance and amount columns. After the dinner I tick who came — the amount is filled in from the expected budget, and I double-click anything that differs. Someone exempt gets Free, which covers every round. Each round shows attendees, collected, paid out and remaining, with the totals over all rounds at the top.

Last, the thank-you e-mail goes out with a table of each round's money and the spreadsheet attached.
